## 5. On-page SEO rules for every new post
- One `<h1>`; logical `<h2>`/`<h3>`.
- Title tag with primary keyword near the front; meta description 150–160 chars ending in value/CTA.
- 3–5 internal links (to comparison tool, `/supplements/`, and 2 related posts).
- `BlogPosting` + `BreadcrumbList` + `FAQPage` JSON-LD (FAQ = 4–5 real "People also ask" questions).
- Add the post to `blog/index.html` and `sitemap.xml` (with `lastmod`).
- Compliance: no therapeutic claims for supplements ("supports", not "treats/cures"); every health article carries the "not veterinary advice" reminder.
